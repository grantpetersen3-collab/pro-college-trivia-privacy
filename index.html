Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro College Trivia ("the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The App may collect the following categories of information:

  • Device Information and Identifiers: Information associated with your device, including device identifiers, may be collected to support advertising, analytics, and app functionality.
  • Usage and Product Interaction Data: Information about how you interact with the App, including usage and interactions with app features, may be collected for analytics and app functionality.
  • Advertising Data: Information associated with advertising activity may be collected and used to provide and measure advertisements.
  • Coarse Location: Approximate location information may be collected and used for advertising and analytics purposes. The App does not collect precise GPS location for these purposes.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Data: Information about crashes, errors, and diagnostics may be collected to help maintain and improve the App.
  • Performance Data: Information about app performance may be collected to help maintain, improve, and analyze the App.

Advertising

Pro College Trivia uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and usage information in accordance with Google's policies and applicable privacy requirements.

Analytics

We may use analytics information to understand how users interact with the App, measure the effectiveness of features, identify technical issues, and improve the App.

How We Use Information

Information collected through the App may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the App;
  • Improve App functionality and performance;
  • Understand how users interact with the App;
  • Detect and resolve crashes and technical problems;
  • Provide and measure advertising; and
  • Support security and prevent fraud or misuse.

Information Sharing

We may share information with third-party service providers that help us operate, analyze, maintain, or advertise the App. These providers may include advertising and analytics services such as Google AdMob.

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain its functionality, perform analytics, comply with legal obligations, and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Pro College Trivia is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Your Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may have choices regarding advertising and certain types of data collection. You may also be able to limit advertising personalization through your device settings or applicable privacy controls.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the App Store listing.
